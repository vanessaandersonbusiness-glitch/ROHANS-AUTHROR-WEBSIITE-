Rohan Anderson

Rohan Anderson was born in Jamaica in 1965. He attended Bethesda All-age School, but his formal education was interrupted during his early adolescent years. His creative journey continued beyond the classroom as he developed his voice as a poet, songwriter, and playwright.

In 1995, Anderson was nominated for Poet of the Year by the International Society of Poets. His poem If Tomorrow Should Ever Come received an international merit award, according to his published author biography.

His debut collection, The Poet Speaks: We Travel This Way, explores the experiences and emotions that connect people across cultures and generations. Its poems touch on subjects including history, romance, and religion, inviting readers to reflect on the many journeys of life.

Biographical information adapted from the publisher-supplied author description of the 2021 Xulon Press edition.
